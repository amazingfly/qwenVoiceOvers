Warm Storybook Heroine

A youthful female lead character with a warm, friendly, natural speaking voice. Clear and expressive with moderate pitch, relaxed pacing, strong emotional range, and an immediately likable personality. She should sound believable during ordinary conversation but become animated naturally when something exciting or magical happens.

REFERENCE TEXT:
The moonlit tide is changing, and I can hear silver bells beneath the waves. Somewhere beyond that glowing gate, our lost kingdom is waiting. Come on, stay close—we've come much too far to turn back now.
