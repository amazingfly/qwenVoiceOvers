Dreamy Moon Mermaid

A young female fantasy voice with a smooth, luminous, slightly dreamy tone. Soft but clearly articulated, with gentle rises and falls in pitch and a mysterious sense of wonder. Emotionally expressive and intimate, as though she knows secrets about moonlight, oceans, and hidden magical places.

REFERENCE TEXT:
The moonlit tide is changing, and I can hear silver bells beneath the waves. Somewhere beyond that glowing gate, our lost kingdom is waiting. Come on, stay close—we've come much too far to turn back now.
