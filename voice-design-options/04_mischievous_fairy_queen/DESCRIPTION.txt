Mischievous Fairy Queen

A youthful female magical character with a light, nimble, playful voice. Bright tone, lively rhythm, expressive pitch changes, quick wit, and a hint of harmless mischief. Warm and lovable rather than shrill. She should sound like a tiny fairy queen who is clever, funny, and constantly thinking of another magical idea.

REFERENCE TEXT:
The moonlit tide is changing, and I can hear silver bells beneath the waves. Somewhere beyond that glowing gate, our lost kingdom is waiting. Come on, stay close—we've come much too far to turn back now.
