Role: extremely old storybook witch with a highly shaped animated character voice. Gender: female. Age: around ninety years old, physically aged but mentally sharp. Persistent vocal posture: narrow forward mouth position with tight nasal resonance and a dry, lightly constricted throat throughout the entire performance. Pitch: thin upper-mid register with unstable little rises, downward cracks and occasional unexpectedly low creaky notes. Resonance: strongly forward and nasal with very little chest resonance. Timbre: papery, brittle, reedy, scratchy, dry, creaky and slightly wheezing. Phonation: irregular aged vocal-fold closure with vocal fry, tiny voice cracks and occasional quavering. Breathing: small audible inhales, dry little huffs and occasional amused breathy chuckles between thoughts. Articulation: sharp consonants, stretched crooked vowels and peculiar emphasis on interesting words. Prosody: highly irregular rhythm with sly pauses, quick bursts and strange melodic turns. Personality: clever, eccentric, mischievous, suspicious and delighted by dangerous magic. Important: this must sound like a deliberately performed classic animated witch character, not merely an elderly woman reading dialogue. Maintain the crooked, reedy, creaky vocal posture through every sentence.
