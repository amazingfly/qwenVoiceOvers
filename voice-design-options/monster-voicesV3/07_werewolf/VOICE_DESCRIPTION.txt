Role: werewolf capable of human language, with an unmistakably animal-shaped speaking voice. Gender: male. Age: mature adult. Persistent vocal posture: tense throat, partially clenched jaw and chest-driven breath pressure throughout all speech, as though human words are being forced through predatory anatomy. Pitch: low baritone with frequent dark dips and restrained rough undertones. Resonance: chest-heavy with tense pharyngeal throat resonance. Timbre: dark, breathy, raspy, feral, rough and slightly growling even when calm. Phonation: compressed with vocal fry, intermittent throat vibration and a subtle low rumble under sustained vowels. Breathing: clearly audible predatory inhalations through the nose, low exhalations and occasional restrained huffs. Articulation: clear words but with heavier consonants, slightly elongated dark vowels and a tight-jawed quality. Prosody: alert pauses, sudden emphasis and restrained melodic range. Personality: vigilant, dangerous, protective and constantly controlling animal instinct. Important: the wolfish quality must persist through normal speech. Do not simply make a raspy action-hero voice.
