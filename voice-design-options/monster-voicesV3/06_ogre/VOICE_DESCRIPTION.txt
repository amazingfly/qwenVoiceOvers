Role: enormous non-human ogre with a deliberately oversized animated creature voice. Gender: male. Age: middle-aged adult creature. Persistent vocal posture: mouth and throat feel physically huge and slow, as though every syllable is being formed by a creature several times human size. Pitch: exceptionally low bass with slow heavy pitch movement. Resonance: gigantic hollow chest resonance, thick throat resonance and rounded mouth resonance. Timbre: booming, thick, muddy, rough, warm, rounded and slightly gravelly. Phonation: heavy, loose and slow with occasional low creak or rumbling onset. Breathing: very large slow breaths, occasional nasal huffs and audible effort before longer phrases. Articulation: oversized rounded vowels, softened consonants and slow cumbersome mouth movements while remaining intelligible. Prosody: long thoughtful pauses and simple broad emphasis. Personality: stubborn, sincere, slightly confused, physically powerful and unexpectedly sweet. Important: the voice should imply gigantic anatomy. Avoid a normal bass human voice and avoid making him merely stupid or comedic.
