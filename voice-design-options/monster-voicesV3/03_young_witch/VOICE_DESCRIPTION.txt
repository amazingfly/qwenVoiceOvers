Persistent vocal posture: bright forward resonance with a slightly pinched magical sharpness and light breath pressure carried through ordinary speech. Timbre: sparkling, breathy, slightly sharp, youthful and faintly mischievous. Phonation: flexible and energetic with occasional airy onset, tiny vocal catches and playful creaky endings. Breathing: quick excited inhales and little amused exhalations when something surprises her. Articulation: crisp, rapid consonants and lively vowel changes. Prosody: highly animated timing, sudden emphasis, little pauses of discovery and quick shifts between curiosity and confidence. Personality: clever, fearless, playful, mischievous and fascinated by magic. Important: maintain a distinct stylized fantasy-witch personality in the actual sound of the voice, not merely in acting emotion. Avoid a polished generic audiobook voice.
