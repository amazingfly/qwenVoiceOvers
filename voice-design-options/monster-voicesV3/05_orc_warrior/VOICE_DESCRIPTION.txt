Role: massive non-human orc warrior with a strongly shaped fantasy-creature voice. Gender: male. Age: mature adult. Persistent vocal posture: broad open throat combined with heavy jaw tension and constant chest-driven roughness. Pitch: very low baritone, broad and heavy rather than merely naturally deep. Resonance: enormous chest resonance with thick throat resonance and a slightly hollow large-bodied quality. Timbre: coarse, leathery, gravelly, dense, blunt and rough-edged. Phonation: compressed and forceful with restrained rasp, occasional low vocal fry and subtle throat rattle. Breathing: powerful audible nose-and-mouth breaths between some phrases, like a very large creature controlling its physical energy. Articulation: hard consonant attacks, wide flattened vowels and slightly shortened word endings. Prosody: blunt rhythmic phrases, limited melodic movement, long downward endings and sudden forceful stresses. Personality: practical, fearless, impatient, disciplined and battle-hardened. Important: the entire speaking mechanism should suggest different anatomy and enormous physical size. Do not simply produce a muscular human man with a deep voice.
