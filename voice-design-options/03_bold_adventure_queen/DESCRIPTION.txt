Bold Adventure Queen

A youthful female fantasy heroine with a lively, confident voice. Clear mid-to-high register, energetic but natural, quick emotional reactions, strong curiosity, and a little fearless determination. She should sound like a young queen who leads her friends into adventures and believes they can solve whatever problem they encounter.

REFERENCE TEXT:
The moonlit tide is changing, and I can hear silver bells beneath the waves. Somewhere beyond that glowing gate, our lost kingdom is waiting. Come on, stay close—we've come much too far to turn back now.
