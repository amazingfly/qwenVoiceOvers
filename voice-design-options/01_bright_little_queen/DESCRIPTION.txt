Bright Little Queen

A young female storybook heroine with a clear, bright, naturally youthful voice. Curious, clever, cheerful and adventurous. Medium-high pitch, expressive melodic intonation, crisp speech, and genuine excitement without sounding exaggerated or cartoonish. She should sound like a brave little queen who is always ready to discover something magical.

REFERENCE TEXT:
The moonlit tide is changing, and I can hear silver bells beneath the waves. Somewhere beyond that glowing gate, our lost kingdom is waiting. Come on, stay close—we've come much too far to turn back now.
