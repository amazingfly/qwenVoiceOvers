Gentle Mermaid Princess

A young female fantasy character with a soft, beautiful, warm voice. Light and clear with a slightly airy quality, gentle melodic intonation, and an affectionate sense of wonder. Calm but not sleepy. She should sound like a kind mermaid princess exploring a magical underwater kingdom.

REFERENCE TEXT:
The moonlit tide is changing, and I can hear silver bells beneath the waves. Somewhere beyond that glowing gate, our lost kingdom is waiting. Come on, stay close—we've come much too far to turn back now.
